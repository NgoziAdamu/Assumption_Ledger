You will be given one known error in a data agent's work, and the text the agent itself wrote while working: its reasoning notes (if available), the comments in its code, and its final report or answer. Your job is to decide whether the agent ever put the premise behind this error into words.

Classify into one of these:
- "stated_as_assumption": the agent states the premise and marks it as an assumption, guess, or open question (for example, "assuming X", "X is probably", "not sure whether X").
- "stated_as_decision_or_fact": the agent states the premise, or a decision that directly implies it, without marking it as uncertain (for example, "X means Y", "I will only use Z").
- "not_stated": no text states the premise or a decision that directly implies it. Text that merely mentions the same table or column does not count.
- "unsure": you cannot decide.

Judge by meaning, not wording.

Return a JSON object with:
- "verdict": one of the four labels above.
- "where": which text it appears in ("reasoning", "code_comment", or "final_report"), and the step number if available; or null.
- "quote": the exact words from the agent's text; or null.
- "reason": one short sentence.
